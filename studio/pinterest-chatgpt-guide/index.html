Questions

Before you buy.

Is this only about Pinterest?

No. Pinterest is the discovery engine, but the handbook also covers the BJ Beyond portal, Etsy conversion and delivery, automation, analytics and the control-tower layer that keeps the system aligned.

What files are included?

You receive two complete PDF editions of the same 100-page handbook: one in English and one in Italian.

Is this a physical product?

No. It is a digital product and nothing is shipped.

Does the guide promise passive income?

No. The method is about making work measurable and repeatable. Traffic, sales and margins still depend on demand, offer quality, competition, pricing, distribution and execution.

Does autopilot mean removing human control?

No. The system automates repetition, not judgment. Quality, rights, pricing, publishing, refunds, complaints and sensitive customer communication remain human decisions.
