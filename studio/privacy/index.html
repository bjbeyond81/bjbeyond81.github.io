1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BJ Beyond / BJBeyondStudio web pages hosted under this Studio portal. It explains how information may be processed when you visit the site or follow external links.

2. Data processed

This website does not currently operate a user account system, newsletter signup, advertising pixel or analytics platform. Technical information such as IP address, browser information, request timestamps and similar server data may nevertheless be processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers as necessary to deliver and secure the website.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Technical data may be processed to provide the website, maintain security, prevent abuse and ensure correct operation. Where processing is necessary to provide a service requested by the visitor or to pursue legitimate security and operational interests, the applicable legal basis is used in accordance with the GDPR.

4. External services and links

The site links to third-party platforms including Etsy and Amazon. When you leave this site, those services process data under their own privacy and cookie policies. This site may also load technical resources such as fonts or images from external content-delivery services; those providers may receive standard technical request data needed to deliver the resource.

5. Amazon affiliate links

Some links on the Amazon Deals page are affiliate links. Clicking them sends you to Amazon. Amazon may process information in accordance with its own privacy and cookie policies. BJ Beyond does not receive your Amazon account credentials or payment details through this website.

6. Retention

Any technical data controlled directly by BJ Beyond, if generated, is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting and legal obligations. Third-party providers determine their own retention periods for data they independently process.

7. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability where applicable, withdrawal of consent where processing is consent-based, and the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
