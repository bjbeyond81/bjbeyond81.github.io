Requirements and demo

Twin requires a modern browser, HTTPS (or localhost for development), Web Crypto, IndexedDB and free storage. Llama mode requires WebGPU and shader-f16. iPhone/iPad default to lightweight SmolLM2 on CPU, which gives simpler answers and is primarily English-oriented. Other CPU devices use Qwen; full Qwen remains selectable on iPhone with higher memory requirements. Performance is not guaranteed on every phone. The first model download needs Internet and around 1 GB, with actual size varying. After complete downloads and offline caching, operation depends on your browser retaining its caches. Try the demo on your device before purchasing: 3 notes and 3 total AI requests.
