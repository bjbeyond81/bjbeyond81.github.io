Privacy, in plain language

Version 1.0 · 29 October 2026 · Technical information about this app. The seller’s details and commercial processing information must be completed before sale.

Your journal stays on your device

Your name, goals, budget, entries, mood, sleep, expenses, embeddings, questions, answers, letters and license code are stored in IndexedDB in an AES-256-GCM encrypted vault. The key is derived from your password using PBKDF2-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations and a random salt. Your password is neither stored nor sent to the seller, and cannot be recovered. Content is decrypted in memory while Twin is open and unlocked.

AI and downloads

Answers and embeddings are computed in your browser using Llama/WebGPU or Qwen/CPU. There are no remote inference APIs, advertising, application analytics or advertising profiles. Hosting providers (such as GitHub Pages) and model providers (Hugging Face and MLC repositories on GitHub) receive ordinary HTTP download requests, including your IP address, browser information and technical logs, under their respective privacy policies. This application does not send them the text of your entries or questions.

Browser storage and voice

Twin uses IndexedDB for your encrypted journal, Cache Storage and IndexedDB for models and offline files, and localStorage for language, theme and AI loading preferences. It sets no application cookies. Dictation starts only at your request and only when your browser exposes mandatory local speech recognition. No remote voice mode is enabled. Your browser may require a local language pack and microphone permission.

Control and retention

Your data remains until you delete it or your browser clears its storage. In Settings, you can export an encrypted JSON file, restore it with the backup password, change your password and delete local data. Changing your password does not change existing exported backups. Delete external backups separately if needed. Keep regular backups: the seller cannot recover your journal. Automatic locking occurs after five minutes in the background or ten minutes of inactivity in the foreground; the operating system may suspend browser timers.

Limits of protection

Encryption protects data at rest, not an already compromised device or an unlocked app. Browsers, extensions, system keyboards and code updates can affect confidentiality. Avoid entering other people’s data without a lawful basis. Twin is not an emergency, diagnosis, therapy or professional advisory service.

Purchases and support

Shopify purchases, payments, tax records and support are separate processing activities from your journal. Before selling, the seller must publish their identity, contacts, purposes, legal bases, recipients, transfers, retention periods and rights request procedures for processing under their responsibility. When seeking technical support, do not send your journal, password or private signing key: use fictional examples.
